# The Petty Manifesto
*Here's to the legally petty.*

**An open letter to everyone with a right they never exercised.**

You know the feeling. The flight cancelled at the gate. The bag that didn't make it. The recall in the pantry you own. The cheque you never cashed. The deadline that came and went while you were waiting on hold. You saw it, you sighed, you let it go — because pursuing it felt bigger than the thing itself.

Let's be honest about why.

It was never the law. The law is written in your favour more often than anyone tells you. It was the attention. It was the form. It was the 40 minutes on hold, the hold music, the chatbot that loops. You were one unanswered letter short of getting paid — and the system knows exactly how many people will stop one letter short.

**This letter is about those people. This product is too.**

## The receipts

Not vibes. Not "compensation specialists say." Statute, regulation, programme, source.

- **EU261/UK261** — €250, €400 or €600 by distance (UK261: £220 / £350 / £520) for qualifying delays and cancellations. It's in the regulation. It does not ask how angry you are.
- **Montreal 1999** — 1,519 SDR for baggage loss or damage; written notice within **7 days** (damage) and **21 days** (delay). Miss the window, lose the claim. Petty always shows the window.
- **DOT automatic refunds (US)** — cancelled or significantly changed flight? The fee refund is supposed to arrive automatically. Checked bag delayed past **12 hours domestic, 15 international ≤12h, 30 international >12h**? The bag fee is supposed to come back to you. Automatically. When it doesn't, that's not fate, that's a process you're owed.
- **FTC refunds** — the Federal Trade Commission mails money to people from enforcement actions. $337.3M in 2024. $392M in 2022. $482M in 2020. People who never sent a letter still deposit those cheques.
- **ATO lost super (AU)** — A$16bn+ in unclaimed super sitting under lost accounts. The search is free.
- **CRA uncashed cheques (CA)** — C$1.8bn, and they never expire. The government is not allowed to keep them. You are allowed to claim them.
- **Marriage Allowance (GB)** — up to £1,260 a year in shifted personal allowance, backdatable four years. Nobody advertises it to you.
- **FSCS (GB) / FDIC (US)** — failed banks compensate depositors up to £85,000 / $250,000 per depositor per bank. Most people learn this after the failure, from the news, too late.
- **PBGC Missing Participants (US)** — a free, searchable database of pension money sitting in nobody's pocket but yours.
- **DOL Abandoned Plan Search (US)**, **Bona Vacantia (GB)**, **ECC-Net (30 European Consumer Centres)** — because the letter you need to write sometimes crosses borders, and borders are not defences.

Every one of those is published, public, attributed, and — here is the petty part — **actionable by you, alone, for free.**

## What everyone sells you instead

A "no-win no-fee" outfit that takes thirty percent of an EU261 payout to send the email you could have sent yourself. A coupon for $3.40 after eight years of litigation you weren't told about. A "compensation expert" whose entire expertise is your desperation, priced as a lead.

Petty does not work that way. Petty never takes a percentage of your recovery — flat subscription, $9 a month, or free gateways, forever, for things that are free anyway.

## What Petty is

Four instruments. One job each. Everything cited, everything dated, everything linked.

- **The Side-Eye** — the agencies, watched. Live recalls, weather hazards, enforcement actions, police and stops data, and the unclaimed-money gateways — flattened into one screen, for your market only.
- **The Receipts** — your household evidence file. How to run your specific pantry jar through the FDA/FSA enforcement corpus, where every match carries its own source and date. Plus the docket watchdog — CourtListener, UK Find Case Law — and a Field Wire of local enforcement incidents. Open, attributable, signed.
- **The Comeuppance** — deterministic statutory engines for the money they owe you: EU261/UK261, Montreal 1999, DOT 254, CTA APPR. Type your route, get your verdict, get the packet. No LLM in the compliance path. The statute computes; Petty just holds the ruler.
- **The Black Book** — every regulator we link to, with its licence, cadence, status (WIRED / PORTAL / BOT-WALLED / UNVERIFIABLE / GATED), the date we checked it, and our link-audit report in public. If a link dies, the Black Book says so. We would rather show you silence than fill the gap with fiction.

## What Petty refuses

Petty is procedural, never performative. One grievance, one lawful channel, one person at a time.

No mass filings. No harassment. No invented deadlines. No guaranteed outcomes — because nobody can guarantee one, and anyone who does is selling something. No success fees, ever. No "trust us." No advice dressed as answers. Petty is information, not legal advice; Petty is not affiliated with any agency, court or regulator; Petty is a relentless, well-sourced reader of the paperwork standing between you and what the statute already says you're owed.
